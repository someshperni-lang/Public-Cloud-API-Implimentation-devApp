Copy the four DEV $metadata JSON files here (from the metadata/ folder of the
DEV dashboard repo), keeping their names, e.g.:

  API_MATERIAL_DOCUMENT_SRV_0001.json
  API_SUPPLIERINVOICE_PROCESS_SRV_0001.json
  API_PURCHASEORDER_PROCESS_SRV_0001.json
  API_PRODUCT_SRV_0001.json

They are bundled into the app and turn on the field checks (unknown fields,
lengths, types) before anything is posted.
